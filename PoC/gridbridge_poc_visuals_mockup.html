Moment 1 · The twin, validated

Feeder F1 constraint map, with AEDC's outage history overlaid

The twin colours every segment by simulated loading. Toggling the overlay drops AEDC's recorded outage clusters onto the map: the model's predicted weak points and the network's real failures line up, without the model ever seeing the outage data.

The demo beat: run the constraint analysis blind, then press the toggle. In this mock, 78% of recorded outages fall inside the twin's predicted constraint zones. AEDC's own records validate the model, not our claims.
Moment 2 · Investment optimisation under a budget

The budget slider: reinforcement projects ranked by cost versus impact

Every identified constraint generates costed reinforcement options from AEDC's unit price list. Each is scored on cost versus impact, ranked, and funded in order until the fictitious budget runs out. Moving the slider redraws the plan live, which is the committee's interactive moment.

Moment 3 · Product 2, on the same backbone

Mini-grid at the PCC: one simulated day, including an islanding event

A typical 850 kWp PV plus 1.2 MWh BESS mini-grid, modelled at its point of common coupling on feeder F1, inside the same twin. At 19:00 the feeder trips in simulation: the site islands, the battery carries local demand, and the site resynchronises at 20:30.
